# LingStack Privacy

LingStack is local-first. The publisher does not receive or store your Agent/Skill definitions, local paths, model credentials, model requests, or usage analytics.

- The public app starts with an empty local library.
- Local discovery runs only after you trigger it.
- Agent and Skill definitions stay on your Mac unless you explicitly export them.
- Model API credentials are stored in macOS Keychain.
- When you test a model connection, LingStack sends the request directly to the endpoint you configured. That provider processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy.
- LingStack does not bundle advertising or analytics SDKs.

For privacy questions, contact n_sir@sina.com.
